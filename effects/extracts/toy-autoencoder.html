Toy · sparse autoencoder

A sparse autoencoder, training in the page

A toy: gradient descent in plain JavaScript on synthetic signals, no GPU, no library, nothing stored or sent. Each square is one hidden unit. It lights as the unit's smoothed activation rises, and under an L1 penalty most of them go dark.

Training

The network is 24 inputs to 64 hidden units to 24 outputs. Every signal is one to three of six fixed random patterns added at random gains, so a good code needs only a few units per signal. The reconstruction error trains the weights; the L1 term penalises every active unit. What you watch is the network settling between the two.

With reduced motion on, training runs at the same rate and the grid repaints every fiftieth step.
