Toast / snackbar

A transient status surface that floats above content, states its message plainly, and leaves. State colour is kept rare and always sits beside a glyph shape and a word, never carried by hue alone. This sheet documents every state and the size and tone variants.

Tones

Neutral is the default. Info, success, warning, and error borrow a single restrained hue on the leading glyph only. The message word carries the meaning so the tone survives without colour.

Live behaviour

These emit a real toast into a polite live region at the foot of the viewport. It enters, times out on a draining progress track, and can be dismissed early. Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Ontology cell: feedback / toast / minimal. Ink and fills are set with alpha colours throughout; the CSS opacity property is used only for entrance and exit keyframes on the whole toast, never to tint a surface. Focus-visible rings are drawn with outline so they survive high-contrast modes.
