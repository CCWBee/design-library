Selection family

Checkbox and radio · Minimal

A reference for the two binary selection controls in the minimal register. Alpha-black ink and fills, a hairline box that fills to solid ink on select, a visible focus ring, and motion held under 200 ms. Every control below is a real, keyboard-operable input.

Checkbox states

The full lifecycle. Default, hover, focus-visible, pressed, checked, indeterminate, and disabled. Hover, focus, and pressed cells are frozen for reference; the rest respond to the pointer and keyboard.

Radio states

The same lifecycle for the single-choice control. A radio has no indeterminate state; otherwise it mirrors the checkbox exactly, drawn as a circle with an ink dot.

Size variants

Three sizes share one geometry. Small for dense tables and toolbars, medium as the default, large for touch and primary questions. The tick, dot, radius, and label all scale together.

In use

Live groups with the controls doing their real job. A radio group for a single choice, a checklist for several. These respond fully to the pointer and keyboard.
