Modal · dialog

Family: surfaces · Register: glass

A frosted panel that keeps its backdrop present: the ground dims and blurs behind it so the layer reads as floating above the page, not replacing it. Tint carries the text contrast the blur removes. Everything below documents the surface, its controls in every state, and the size variants.

Surface

The default panel: a translucent tint over a live blur, a 1px top-edge highlight, a hairline border, and a two-stop soft shadow. Shown at rest on the backdrop tile.

Panel title

Body text sits on the tinted film, not on raw blur. The tint is heavier on a text-bearing panel than on a purely decorative one, so type clears its contrast floor.

Notes

  • Ontology cell: surfaces / modal-dialog / glass.
  • Colour and dimming are set with alpha values, never the CSS opacity property.
  • Focus is visible on both button kinds and the close control, on glass and on tile.
  • An @supports fallback swaps the blur for a near-opaque tint where backdrop-filter is absent.
  • prefers-reduced-transparency drops the blur and keeps a plain dim; prefers-reduced-motion stills the spinner and transitions.
