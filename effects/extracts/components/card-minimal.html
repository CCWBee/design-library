Card

surfaces · card · minimal

A hairline sheet that holds one idea. Structure comes from space and a single 10% border, not from shadow. The interactive variant behaves as one control: it hovers, takes a visible focus ring, presses, disables and loads.

Default

Focus-visible Tab to the live cards to see the real ring.
Pressed Fill deepens, surface sinks 1px.
Disabled Ink drops to metadata rung, not the opacity property.
Loading
Loading Slow opacity pulse, no shimmer sweep.
