Switch

A binary toggle: a pill track and a travelling knob in neutral alpha-black. On carries a solid ink track; off is a light neutral fill with a hairline edge. One reference, every state it can hold.

States

Each control shown in its off and on position where the state has both. Ink and fills are set with alpha colours, so a disabled control reads as muted without fading its label out of contrast.
