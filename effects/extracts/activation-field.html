Activation field

Sparse features, lit where you point.

Every dot is one feature with its own slow breath. Only a few breathe with any strength. The pointer is an activation site, and any element on the page can fire a region of its own through a small script call.

Script API

Call ActivationField.activate(x, y, strength, ms) with x and y in 0..1 of the viewport, origin top left, to light a region for a moment. Strength runs 0..1 and the site lives for ms milliseconds with a short attack and a long decay. The call returns a function that ends the site early. Up to six sites run at once; a seventh replaces the weakest.

ActivationField.activate(0.82, 0.24, 1, 2400)

Hover or focus a control to fire its region for 1400 ms; press to hold it for longer. Both go through the same call, and the readout above counts the sites as they live and expire.
